Anua · Japan2024 – 2025Research lead

Vitamin C serum renewal

Before deciding what to make, I looked at what was already selling. I compared the sales of more than 30 competitor products across Qoo10, Rakuten, Amazon and @cosme, then read their reviews to find why customers chose them.

Interviews pointed to a preference for high concentration, but the people we spoke to were mostly heavy beauty users. I added a survey of over 2,000 consumers, including casual buyers, to check it.

  • Tested3 concepts, compared with ANOVA
  • FoundThe key claim, and 10% as the lowest level consumers read as "high concentration"
  • Result170,000 units in the Qoo10 Mega Sale debut, the brand's best launch
From serum to summer gel cream, validated in3 weeks
Anua · Japan2025Project lead

A product saved by a new category

A serum planned for the second half of the year tested poorly on purchase intent. Reading the interviews again, I noticed people kept praising the texture itself. The formula was right; the category was wrong.

I proposed relaunching it as a summer gel cream and ran four consumer sessions myself, from recruiting and setting up the room to moderating in Japanese, with product managers watching people use it.

  • Sessions4 rounds, 28 consumers, no agency
  • ChangedFinish, packaging, usage routine and claims
  • ResultExpanded to a global SKU; strong offline sales that lifted the whole line and won shelf space
